Silent Hill Producer Says Players Have “Largely Embraced” Moving Beyond the Iconic Town

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

If you told any Silent Hill fan, much less a horror aficionado, that they would get three well-reviewed titles in the franchise in three years, they wouldn’t have believed you. But all those years after that first Silent Hill Transmission, it’s incredible how well the series has bounced back, whether it’s the Silent Hill 2 remake, Silent Hill f or the recently released Silent Hill: Townfall.

Konami hasn’t been shy about future projects, with series producer Motoi Okamoto discussing potential second and third waves. Bloober Team’s Silent Hill 1 remake is doubtless included, but what has the first wave taught about which proposals should move forward? Okamoto told us that, “We’ve been observing a lot of feedback from players. There have been both positive and negative reactions to moving the setting beyond the town of Silent Hill itself, but overall, I feel that players have largely embraced that direction.

“Going forward, I hope players will look forward to seeing where in the world we might go next, and which studios around the world we might collaborate with.”

In terms of gameplay, however, the team is seemingly open to a variety of approaches as long as they maintain the “ebb and flow of tension” that Okamoto believes is “very important” for horror games.

“Silent Hill 2 strikes a good balance between moments of heightened tension and moments where that tension eases, while Silent Hill f gives players relatively more room to breathe. I feel that the gameplay in both titles was well received.

“Silent Hill: Townfall, by comparison, places greater emphasis on sustained tension, and my impression is that this resulted in more divided reactions to the gameplay. It was a valuable lesson in how important it is to carefully balance those moments of tension with opportunities for the player to breathe.” Which makes you wonder if it’s going to return to the approaches seen in Silent Hill 2 and f for the future.

Time will tell, so stay tuned for updates. In the meantime, the first cast members of Silent Hill 1 remake have reportedly been revealed, including Gabrielle Fritz (Wolfenstein 2: The New Colossus, Battlefield 2042) and Jade Matthew (Dragon Nest). Their roles remain unknown, though, so keep your eyes peeled for the official announcement.

GTA 6 Lets You “Have Your Life in the Game,” Says Rockstar

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

While it lacks a leveling system, loot tiers and other tropes you would typically associate with a role-playing game, Grand Theft Auto 6 is certainly closer to the genre than previous titles. The sheer choice in role-playing Lucia, Jason or both, whether building bonds, going to the gym (and keeping a steady sleep schedule), or the consequences of your actions, is overwhelming.

“You can really make the game what you want it to be at the time, whether that’s more of a roleplay experience where you’re buying cigarettes and beer in the shop and going on the train for the day, or going to a club,” said head of development Aaron Garbutt to Edge Magazine (via GamesRadar). “You can have your life in the game.”

With Rockstar aiming to make this the most realistic GTA game yet, it can be a lot to handle. You can’t even carjack anything as easily as previous games or escape the police’s attention by simply avoiding them for extended periods. Even cars must be refuelled. Co-studio head Rob Nelson noted how, “You don’t want to feel like you’re foisting things on the player and then have them reject those things. It’s more about trying to let people lean into the way they want to play.”

That sounds a lot like what senior VP of narrative Rupert Humphries said last month about realism for the sake of immersion and foregoing that for “what’s convenient for the player.” But for those who vibe with that balance, there are a lot of options. For example, head of technology Phil Hooker talked about the bus systems and how he’s “memorized all of the routes,” because he likes taking public transport instead of driving.

Launching November 19th for Xbox Series X/S and PS5, Grand Theft Auto 6 retails for $79.99, with the $99.99 Ultimate Edition unlocking access to several exclusive stores and other goodies. Head here for more details and learn how the day/night cycle is longer than in GTA 5.

The free-to-play game is already releasing on PC, iOS, and Android on December 4, 2026, but it never hurts to have more cute and cozy content on your Switch.

"In my short time of about five hours with a preview of the closed beta, Petit Planet offered a charming, "The Little Prince"-themed take on Animal Crossing: New Horizons with an emphasis on clear progression and multiplayer. While these are two things some seemed to crave and were left empty in ACNH, the Genshin Impact and Honkai: Star Rail studio may offer an answer – with potential caveats."

The special update also revealed a bunch of chances for players to score pre-registration prizes as well as a "Launch a Planet" event on the game's official website that starts today.

Tumor index can identify breast cancer patients' drug response

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →
Researchers at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU) have developed an imaging biomarker, the Tumour Heterogeneity Index (THI), which enables noninvasive assessment of breast cancer patients' response to AKT inhibitors before treatment, helping clinicians identify patients most likely to benefit from therapy. The study found that patients with a high THI achieved an estimated pathological complete response (pCR) rate of 56.1%, significantly higher than that of patients with a low THI. The technology could help patients receive the most appropriate treatment at an earlier stage, reduce unnecessary side effects and enable clinicians to better tailor treatment strategies to individual patients.
