Heat Director Michael Mann Hospitalized After Accidentally Shooting Himself in the Leg

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Heat director Michael Mann was hospitalized with minor injuries after accidentally shooting himself in the leg at a Los Angeles gun range on Halloween.

TMZ confirmed the news with local authorities and the filmmaker's representative today. Mann was reportedly sent to the hospital for treatment and is in the process of being released.

"On Saturday, at a gun range, a weapon accidentally discharged while holstered resulting in a superficial injury to film director Michael Mann," his representative said this morning. "He was treated and is being released."

Mann is known for his role in developing some of the biggest crime dramas of the last 40 years and, as noted by TMZ, is a highly experienced target shooter himself. The site described the incident as having occurred during a training exercise of some kind, with its sources saying the 83-year-old director had no intention of harming himself. Additional details are unclear.

An ambulance ride to the hospital is probably not how Mann wanted to spend his Halloween in Los Angeles. While the accident took a bite out of his weekend, the filmmaker is currently putting the pieces of his long-awaited Heat follow-up together behind the scenes.

Our first big confirmation that the ball was rolling for Heat 2 arrived in February of this year when The Dark Knight star Christian Bale confirmed he was set to start filming the movie in Chicago soon. Inception's Leonardo DiCaprio boarded the project in July, with both he and Bale then reportedly said to play Chris Shiherlis and Vincent Hanna, respectively. Filming was expected to begin in November, this month, at the time.

Mann is also responsible for movies like Ferrari, Public Enemies, and Manhunter. Also expected to appear in Heat 2 are Adolescence's Stephen Graham and The Bear's Ebon Moss-Bachrach. Story details about the follow-up aren't completely clear, though we do know that it will have Mann's novel of the same name to lean on.

While we wait to hear more from Mann, you can check out IGN's 8/10 review of Heat's Blu-ray release. You can also read our 7/10 review of Ferrari. Last month, the director premiered a director's cut of his 2006 film adaptation, Miami Vice. He shed light on his most recent changes during a Q&A at Beyond Fest.

Final Fantasy 7 Revelation Has 3-Person Synergy Attacks But Only in “Emotional or Dramatic Moments”

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Lifestyle  |  Read Source →

Final Fantasy 7 Remake’s combination of real-time combat with pause, harnessing the classic’s ATB in unique ways, resulted in some of the most enjoyable RPG combat out there. It’s hard to believe it could get better, but Rebirth upped the ante significantly, especially with new Synergy Attacks (which first debuted in Episode INTERmission).

By comparison, Final Fantasy 7 Revelation doesn’t add too much to these established systems (not counting FITS, new party members and mounted combat). However, director Naoki Hamaguchi told PlayStation Access that there are three-person Synergy Attacks. If that sounds utterly broken, well, it would be, which is why there’s a catch.

“Talking about this with [battle director Teruki] Endo-san, obviously there are normal abilities. Synchronized abilities, the Limit Breaks, and so we’d be adding to that as well. And if we added in the three-person Synergy Attack, then obviously that’s going to be the strongest one at the top of the tower, and everyone’s going to want to use that, which will make battles monotonous.

“So we decided not to make it something that could be used at any time. Instead, we decided to make it more dependent on the gameplay experience, where there may be a particularly emotional or dramatic moment. So I hope you’ll be looking forward to when they may pop up.”

Making them feel more special makes sense, and Final Fantasy is no stranger to such dramatic moments. Remember the climactic battle against Sephiroth, which involved using a special move (a moment recreated in stunning fashion with Final Fantasy 7 Advent Children’s climax)? Of course, when you start thinking about memorable combat moments with three characters, the possibilities become almost endless.

Final Fantasy 7 Revelation launches on April 7th, 2027, for Xbox Series X/S, PS5, PC, and Nintendo Switch 2. It’s a massive adventure and could easily take over 100 hours to complete, per Hamaguchi, but that’s only the beginning, as multiple playthroughs are apparently required to see everything. And that’s not even getting into the post-launch expansions that focus on Sephiroth and Vincent Valentine.

DOOM Studio is “Working More Closely” With MachineGames, “Really Bullish on What We Have Ahead”

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

id Software of DOOM fame may be nowhere near its former size and scale, but work certainly hasn’t stopped. From going “all in” on id Tech to reportedly working on a new Doom game, it’s also “working more closely” with MachineGames than ever before.

Studio co-director Marty Stratton revealed this during a recent press event (via IGN) for DOOM: The Dark Ages – Revelations. The initial question revolved around id Software’s status after the mass layoffs. “We’re doing great. Not to handwave the [layoffs] away…everybody knows we had about half of our studio exit as part of that. But there was a very deliberate effort to make sure that we were incredibly viable coming out of that across everything we do, across game development and the technology department.”

He then revealed, “We’re working more closely with MachineGames than ever. We’ve always had a very close partnership with them, but I think we’re at a point with technology development that both teams are incredibly talented and have incredibly talented engineers, and we’re really on an amazing path that had started pre-[layoffs]. We kicked this off earlier this year. We’re really excited about that. We’re both really, really bullish on what we have ahead.”

The obvious assumption is that id Software is helping out with the rumored Wolfenstein 3. Reports of casting calls emerged last February, with Windows Central further corroborating that it’s in development. A live-action show is also allegedly in the works with Amazon’s Fallout producers at the helm. Of course, there’s no information on when either will premiere, but an announcement in the coming year wouldn’t be too shocking.

The real question is whether they’re collaborating on the next Indiana Jones title, if it’s in development. More titles are allegedly in the works after the success of Indiana Jones and the Great Circle, but since the rumor first emerged in December 2024, there hasn’t been a peep.

Stay tuned for updates, and in the meantime, check out our review of DOOM: The Dark Ages – Revelations. Even in a packed year like this, it’s perhaps the best first-person shooter campaign out there.

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt’s Expansion Pricing Was About Providing “Quality at a Good Value”

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

CD Projekt RED has already made a significant return to The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt with its free Remastered update. However, Songs of the Past is a much bigger deal because it marks the first expansion for the open-world RPG since 2016’s Blood and Wine. While the developer hasn’t announced pricing, you can probably look back at its Expansion Pass, which over-delivered on value.

In fact, that’s what MP1st did with global community and social media director Marcin Momot. When asked what motivated this pricing, he said, “When we launched The Witcher 3, it was our first open-world game and our first real attempt at putting our storytelling philosophy to the test in such a wide and free setting. It was our most ambitious game at the time, by far, and I think the expansion pass was also part of that ambition.”

After the base game’s lofty bar, CD Projekt RED knew it had to over-deliver with its expansions, even with the affordable entry fee. “Most importantly though, it was all about the players. For those who supported us by buying the expansion pass for The Witcher 3, it really felt like they were putting their trust in us to expand the game in a meaningful way. I think it was a way to thank them for doing so and being there to take that journey with us.

“Above all, we wanted to provide quality at a good value. When we were finishing development on The Witcher 3 and thinking about expansions, we always said we wanted to create something that represented a real value for money. I think it’s safe to say we hit the mark,” said Momot.

It’s hard to disagree. Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine are considered some of the best expansions in gaming history. The former delivered an incredible narrative centered on Olgierd von Everec and the mysterious Gaunter O’Dimm. The latter introduced a new region, Tussant, and activities (while also sporting its own memorable story).

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Songs of the Past launches in 2027 for Xbox Series X/S, PS5, PC, and Nintendo Switch 2. It reached an astounding 900,000 wishlists after its Gamescom reveal. Though its release date remains unknown, CD Projekt RED confirmed it would focus on Dandelion and provide closure to his story with Geralt. Those who haven’t finished Blood and Wine should probably do so now – you may be treated to a few differences in the new expansion.

Taika Waititi Blasts Critics as 'Irrelevant, Out of Touch, Obsolete' Following Klara and the Sun's Negative Reviews

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Marvel filmmaker Taika Waititi has called critics "sad lost souls who couldn't achieve their dreams" after his latest film, Klara and the Sun, premiered with negative reviews.

The director behind projects like Jojo Rabbit and Thor: Love and Thunder unleashed his true feelings in a post on Instagram. Tied to an image shared by ScreenRant earlier this week that reflects audience reception to the live-action book adaptation, Waititi posted an emoji-filled message taking aim at the reviewers who walked away from his new movie unimpressed.

"I haven't read a review of anything aimed at me or my peers in almost 15 years," he said, along with a series of emojis. "But I do listen to the people."

Klara and the Sun's critical reception has struggled in the wake of its October 23, 2026, release date. While Rotten Tomatoes has recorded mostly positive reactions from viewers at 74% favorability, its Tomatometer has sunk to a 'rotten' 26%. Its IMDb Metascore is similarly dire at a negative 30 out of 100 after 27 reviews. IGN did not review the movie.

Waititi said he's strayed from reading reviews for years, but that didn't stop him from pointing his 2.8 million Instagram followers to those who didn't enjoy the story his Klara and the Sun was trying to tell.

"So, this Halloween, let's spare a thought for those sad lost souls who couldn't achieve their dreams of becoming artists, and thus settled, and became critics. The same people who also hated Jojo Rabbit," he added with a laughing-crying-face and shrug emoji.

Jojo Rabbit is Waititi's 2019 project and his first since turning heads with Thor: Ragnarok in 2017. The writer, director, actor, and producer suggested critics weren't fond of the World War II-era dark comedy, though what negative reaction he's referring to here isn't quite clear.

Jojo Rabbit boasts an impressive 81% critical rating on Rotten Tomatoes. Although its IMDb score isn't quite as high at a middling 58, some of the reviews listed there are extremely positive, including an A- from Entertainment Weekly and an 85 from The Wrap. IGN gave the film an 8.5/10.

"Klara and the Sun introduces audiences to Klara (Jenna Ortega), an Artificial Friend who wants nothing more than to find the perfect home," an official description for the film from Sony Pictures said. "When Klara meets Josie (Mia Tharia), each immediately senses a kindred spirit in the other. Josie has a fraught relationship with her mother (Amy Adams) and they’ve suffered great loss, but Klara’s innocent wonder and unwavering loyalty begin to heal the family and bring light to Josie’s complicated world."

While Waititi has had much to say about how critics have responded to Klara and the Sun, the films seems to have struggled to grab the attention of the average moviegoer, even if those who have seen it generally had a decent experience. According to Box Office Mojo, the movie adaptation of Kazuo Ishiguro's novel of the same name has only managed to pull in a dismal 1.7 million worldwide after two weekends in theaters.

Klara and the Sun was also expected to debut in around 2,000 theaters last week before Sony seemingly rolled back that plan with an 800-theater debut, per Collider. Deadline reported that the company chose to stop reporting box office earnings for the film this weekend, too, with its budget reportedly set somewhere around $20 million - $40 million.

Waititi signed off his Instagram post, saying, "Irrelevant. Out of touch. Obsolete. In the bin." He added a trash can emoji, a crying face emoji, and a heart hands emoji.

Waititi was previously set to helm a Star Wars movie, but after six years of attempting to get the film off the ground, he announced the project was "on ice" earlier this month. He once said his take on the universe was trying to "harness a little bit more of the fun from" the original trilogy. What's next for Klara and the Sun remains unclear.

Stranger Than Heaven’s Current-Gen Exclusivity Allows for More Characters On-Screen, per RGG Studio

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

RGG Studio is no stranger to ambitious games. Look no further than Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth with its massive story and playtime. However, Stranger Than Heaven, which spans 50 years and multiple eras, could one-up it, at least in terms of sheer scope. Building everything from the ground up makes it all the more ambitious.

To deliver on that quality bar, the team left previous-gen consoles behind completely. As producer Hiroyuki Sakamoto noted to IGN at the recent Gamescom Asia x Thailand Game Show, “We set out to make Stranger Than Heaven as a completely brand-new game, with the goal of creating something entirely fresh while putting forth the absolute best quality we can produce right now. [So] we decided to drop previous-gen platforms like the PS4 and focus on creating an experience best enjoyed natively on current-gen consoles like the PS5.”

Fans of the Yakuza and Like a Dragon franchise may immediately note the difference in the number of characters displayed. Their overall fidelity has increased as well. “In the past, hardware constraints meant limiting the number of characters we could display on screen at once, among other things, we had to give up on. With Stranger Than Heaven, by fully leveraging the PS5’s capabilities, we were able to showcase more characters on screen simultaneously than ever before and raise character quality to the next level.”

Given that you’ll be involved in gang fights with multiple characters on-screen at once, both on your side and against, it makes sense. However, RGG Studio may have taken this approach for protagonist Makoto Daito’s later journey as a showman. After working with composers, recruiting musicians, hiring singers (each with their own stories) and more, he’ll organize performances in various venues.

Based on gameplay, these can get fairly packed, with Daito still having tasks. Such fidelity may have been too much for previous-gen consoles to handle while still offering reliable performance. Of course, there’s the overall scale, though RGG Studio hasn’t outlined how it compares to its biggest environments.

Stranger Than Heaven launches on January 15th, 2027, and based on all the trailers showcasing its various years, especially the opening in 1915 Kure, it’s shaping up to be something special.
