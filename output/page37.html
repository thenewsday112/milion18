You Can Unlock Lots Of Free Stuff In Pokemon Go’s Halloween 2026 Event

📅 Oct 27, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Halloween may be over, but Pokemon Go is still celebrating the spooky holiday with its annual Halloween event. This year's festivities take place across two parts that run into early November, each of which will offer new Pokemon, Research tasks, and other bonuses.

To help you prepare, here's everything you need to know about Pokemon Go's Halloween 2026 event.

Halloween Part I

Event Schedule

The first part of this year's Halloween event kicks off at 10 AM local time on October 27 and runs until 10 AM local time on November 1.

Featured Pokemon

Like previous years, a handful of new costumed Pokemon will make their debut during the Halloween festivities. Part I will introduce three costume-wearing monsters to catch:

  • Pikachu wearing a hat and capelet
  • Zubat wearing a top hat
  • Sinistea wearing a bow

You can encounter the aforementioned Pokemon in the wild or as one-star Raid bosses. Shiny variants will also be appearing throughout the event.

In addition, some costume-wearing Pokemon from previous years will appear in the wild once again, as will other creepy monsters like Duskull and Misdreavus.

Wild Encounters

  • Zubat wearing a top hat
  • Misdreavus
  • Duskull
  • Piplup wearing a Halloween Mischief costume
  • Drifblim wearing a Halloween Mischief costume
  • Pumpkaboo

Raid Bosses

One-Star

  • Pikachu wearing a hat and capelet
  • Sinistea wearing a bow

Timed And Field Research

Pokemon Go is offering a free Timed Research for all players. Log in before Part I ends on November 1 to claim the Research, which will be completed automatically and net you the following rewards:

  • Rare Candies (6)
  • An encounter with Pikachu wearing a hat and capelet

Some Halloween-themed Field Research tasks will also be available during the event. You can claim these by spinning PokeStops, and they'll offer various rewards when completed, including additional encounters with the featured Pokemon.

Chandelure Super Mega Raid Day

A spooky new Mega-Evolved Pokemon is making its debut on Halloween: Mega Chandelure.

The ghost/fire type will appear as a Super Mega Raid boss on October 31 from 2-5 PM local time, marking your first chance to get this Mega-Evolved form in Pokemon Go. Not only that, but you'll have increased odds of encountering shiny Chandelure from these Raids, and any Chandelure that you catch will have Mega Level 1 already unlocked.

A few other bonuses will be available during this mini-event. The number of Remote Raids you can join is being increased to 20 for a limited time, and you'll be able to get up to six free Raid Passes when you spin the Photo Disc at Gyms.

Bonuses

  • Remote Raid limit increased to 20 (October 30-31)
  • Receive up to six free Raid Passes from spinning Gym Photo Discs
  • Chandelure caught from Super Mega Raids will have Mega Level 1 unlocked
  • Increased chance of encountering shiny Chandelure from Super Mega Raids

Bonuses

Like past years, Pokemon Go will receive a spooky makeover during the Halloween event. The usual nighttime background music will be replaced by a remix of the infamous Lavender Town theme, and Gyms and PokeStops will be decked out in Halloween decorations.

On top of that, the game will offer its own version of trick-or-treating. As you play, you'll periodically notice gift boxes on the map. Tap on these and you'll receive candy or initiate an encounter with a costumed Pokemon.

Finally, a few additional bonuses will unlock as you level up the event's Go Pass. Hit rank 10 and you'll receive extra candy for catching Pokemon, and you'll earn bonus Stardust on top of that if you reach rank 20.

Go Pass Tier 1 (Rank 10)

  • 2x candy for catching Pokemon
  • Go Pass Deluxe: 3x candy for catching Pokemon

Go Pass Tier 2 (Rank 20)

  • 2x Stardust for catching Pokemon
  • Go Pass Deluxe: 3x Stardust for catching Pokemon

Halloween Part II

Event Schedule

This year's Halloween festivities will continue into early November. Part II begins at 10 AM local time on November 1 and runs until 8 PM local time on November 5.

Featured Pokemon

Like Part I, a new Pokemon is debuting during the second leg of this year's Halloween event: shiny Greavard. This marks the first time you'll have a chance to encounter this shiny variant in Pokemon Go.

Mega Sableye is also getting a power boost. Beginning with Part II, you'll be able to power up the Pokemon's Mega Level up to Super Max Level, which means it'll have access to an additional Charged Attack–Night Shade+--while it's Mega Evolved.

A few other themed Pokemon will also be available during the event. Costumed versions of Gengar and Rowlet will appear in the wild, as will ghost types like Litwick.

Wild Encounters

  • Gengar wearing a Spooky Festival costume
  • Litwick
  • Rowlet wearing a Pokemon Halloween costume
  • Greavard

Raid Bosses

One-Star

  • Pikachu wearing a hat and capelet
  • Sinistea wearing a bow

Timed And Field Research

Another free Timed Research will be available during the second part of this year's Halloween event. Log in before November 5 to claim the Timed Research, which will be completed automatically and offer the following rewards:

  • Rare Candies (6)
  • An encounter with Sinistea wearing a bow

A variety of Field Research tasks will also be available. These can be claimed from PokeStops and will reward you with encounters with the event's featured Pokemon upon completion.

This bonus recipe has nothing to do with Thai food but it is one of the best things I make, so I have to share it with my biggest supporters. Brown butter protein rice Krispie treats...and before you say "are you serious?," let me tell you why these are different and worth your time.

Whenever I have a container of these sitting at home, which is often, when people come over, I offer them a piece, and predictably, the next thing you know half the container is gone! They're not too sweet, a little salty, extra milky from the protein powder, and extra nutty aroma from the brown butter and pumpkin seeds. They are highly addictive. People have a hard time having just one!

The protein powder was a happy accident. With a picky eating child, I wanted to find a way to sneak extra protein into every little thing he eats. What I did not expect was that the milkiness from the whey protein made these treats actually taste better! So whether or not you want to make these higher protein, I would recommend adding it, but if you don't want to go buy a whole tub of protein for this, you can also omit. I have detailed notes about the right protein powder to use and what you can use instead in the recipe card!

They're perfect for bringing to get togethers, especially if kids will be around!

Epic Brown Butter Rice Krispie Treats

Rice Krispie Treats like no other, these are nutty, milky, not too sweet and a little salty. Studded with crunchy pumpkin seeds, they are highly addictive! Less sweet and higher protein than regular ones! Course Dessert Cuisine American Prep Time 15 minutes minutes Cook Time 10 minutes minutes Total Time 20 minutes minutes Servings 32 pieces

Ingredients

  • 6 cups Rice Krispie cereal see note 1
  • ½ cup toasted pumpkin seeds or sub other kinds of nuts or seeds
  • ¼ cup hemp hearts optional, feel free to sub other small seeds like chia seeds or sesame seeds, see note 2
  • 1 scoop unflavoured whey protein powder optional, see note 3
  • ¾ teaspoon table salt or another fine grain salt
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • 9.5 oz marshmallows mini ones dissolve more quickly but regular size ones are fine
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
US Customary - Metric

Instructions

  • Prepare a 9x13 pan lined with parchment paper, or whatever containers you want.
  • Combine the rice krispie cereal, pumpkin seeds, hemp hearts, protein powder, salt and mix well. 6 cups Rice Krispie cereal, ½ cup toasted pumpkin seeds, ¼ cup hemp hearts, 1 scoop unflavoured whey protein powder, ¾ teaspoon table salt
  • In a pot or a large wok, ideally nonstick, add the butter and melt over medium heat. Once melted and the bubbling starts to subside, stir frequently until the milk solids at the bottom of the pan is dark brown. Do not walk away, it burns fast! ½ cup unsalted butter
    • I highly recommend you watch the video tutorial for this process if you've never browned butter before.
  • Once the butter has browned, immediately add the marshmallows and quickly stir to mix and then turn the heat down to LOW. Add the vanilla and keep stirring until the marshmallows are mostly melted but still a little bit lumpy. 9.5 oz marshmallows, 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Remove the pan from the heat, add the cereal mixture and quickly toss with two spatula until the rice krispies are evenly coated in the marshmallow. (If using an electric stove, move the pan off the element during mixing).
    • If for some reason the mixture has cooled and firmed up before mixing is complete, you can put it back on LOW heat to soften it and make the mixing easier.
  • Transfer into the prepared pan and smooth out the rice krispies without compressing it in order to keep it light and airy. It will not fill a 9x13 pan completely, so you'll have space around the edges to use your hand/spatula to press the edges to straighten them (press through the parchment, don't touch the treats with your bare hands!) Allow to cool and then cut into pieces once cooled. Store in an airtight container, they last a couple of weeks at room temp.

Video

Notes

  1. If you're not using Kellogg's brand rice Krispie cereal, use the cup measure instead of the weight. I tried this with gluten free rice cereal to make a gluten free version, but because they are much heavier and denser, 180g did not provide enough volume for the amount of marshmallows in this recipe. 
  2. I add hemp hearts for a little extra protein, fiber and omega 3. You will not taste them. Feel free to omit, or sub other seeds like chia seeds or sesame seeds. 
  3. The whey protein powder gives a lovely milkiness to these treats (I use Nutrabolics hydrolyzed whey protein, but you can use other brands). Do not use flavoured protein powder because it will make it too sweet. If you don't have it, you can try adding 3 tablespoons of milk powder and I think it should provide the same milky flavour! Do not add any more protein powder in the attempt to make these higher protein; I tried it, and it just makes it too dry as the protein powder absorbs moisture.
