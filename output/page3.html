A Salmonella outbreak in the United Kingdom linked to eggs from Ukraine has sickened almost 80 people across two years.

The strain is associated with 40 illnesses in England in 2026 and 36 in 2025. 

As part of a large Salmonella Enteritidis outbreak with 700 patients connected to imported eggs, microbiological testing of nearly 3,000 eggs was undertaken by the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA). This sampling detected Salmonella Enteritidis in a batch of eggs from Ukraine and a separate outbreak.

Affected eggs came from Yasensvit and are stamped with the code 3UA1012066. The Food Standards Agency (FSA) and Food Standards Scotland (FSS) asked businesses to stop using them.

Yasensvit, part of the Ovostar Union Group, is one of the largest producers of eggs and egg products in Europe and operates in international markets. The company previously urged people to avoid drawing what it called “premature conclusions” until all the facts were established.

Detection of Salmonella
In October, Salmonella Enteritidis was detected in three samples of six pooled eggs each from the Ukrainian producer. Nine isolates were sent to the reference laboratory and it was confirmed via whole genome sequencing (WGS) that they were the same strain of Salmonella Enteritidis and associated with human infections.

The date of sampling was Sept. 23, 2026. Eggs were reportedly laid and packaged on Aug. 23, with an expiry date of Sept. 19. This means samples were taken four days after the best before date.

There are 78 confirmed cases with sample dates between Dec. 17, 2024, and Sept. 26, 2026. Wales has two cases while the rest live in England. In total, 32 are from London and 24 are from the East of England. Cases range from younger than 1 to 99 years old with a median age of 30. Across all age groups, 39 are female.

There was one outbreak in June 2026 with six confirmed patients linked to a restaurant in Yorkshire and Humber where non-UK eggs were used for certain dishes.

The State Service of Ukraine on Food Safety and Consumer Protection said it would conduct an inspection at the company in Kyiv including taking product samples for lab testing to verify information received from UK authorities.

Cyclospora update
UKHSA, Public Health Scotland and Public Health Wales are also investigating travel-related Cyclospora infections.

Reported cases decreased in August and September, after a peak in June and July 2026. There were 49 illnesses in June, 47 in July, 10 in August, and seven in September. 

Between April and September, 135 cases of cyclosporiasis were reported with 81 from England, 43 from Scotland and 11 from Wales. The median age of patients was 47, and more than 60 percent were female. It was previously reported that between April and mid-July, 67 people had fallen sick.

Of the 95 cases with travel information available, 88 had been to Mexico, with Riviera Maya remaining the primary region. Patients stayed in different hotels and consumed a variety of food and drinks as part of all-inclusive holiday packages.

There were 87 cyclosporiasis cases in England in 2025 and 124 in 2024. Cyclospora cayetanensis is a parasite that does not naturally occur in the UK. Travelers to Mexico and other areas where the infection is more common can reduce their risk by following good food and water hygiene measures, including drinking bottled water and eating thoroughly cooked food.

Sister Wives’ Christine Details the 'Real' End of Meri and Kody's Relationship

📅 Nov 02, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Christine Brown had a heart-to-heart with Meri Brown on Sister Wives about the end of her marriage to Kody Brown.

“I loved my leaving process,” Christine, 54, told Meri, 55, during the Sunday, November 1, episode of the TLC series, referring to her split from Kody, 57, in November 2021, which came less than two years before Meri announced her breakup from Kody in January 2023.

Christine explained that it seemed to be different for Meri, who was Kody’s first of four wives. “I saw that he just drifted you along,” Christine said, suggesting that Meri and Kody’s 2014 legal divorce appeared to be their undoing.

“He did, because was never direct with me and I was willing to figure it out,” Meri confessed, noting that she did a “freaking legal divorce” decades before their actual breakup “thinking things were going to be OK and they were not.”

Sister Wives' Kody Admits He 'Didn't Love' His Wives But Wouldn't 'Leave'

Meri claimed that Kody “was nice to me for, I don’t know, maybe a month” after that big gesture.

Meri recalled that in 2014, four years after Kody spiritually wed his fourth wife, Robyn Brown, he and Meri got legally divorced “because they were talking about him wanting to adopt Robyn’s kids.”

Meri and Kody legally tied the knot in 1990 before he spiritually wed Janelle Brown, Christine and Robyn, in 1993, 1994 and 2010, respectively. (Like Meri and Christine, Janelle, 57, eventually left Kody as well, confirming her separation in December 2022.)

However, in 2014, Meri agreed to legally separate from their collective spouse and then Kody and Robyn, 48, got legally married, making the adoption of her three children from a prior marriage “easier.”

Kody Brown. TLC/YouTube

“Even back then I was like, ‘It’s a mistake. It’s a mistake,’” Christine recalled during Sunday’s episode. “I know that having Kody and Robyn be able to adopt Robyn’s kids was Meri’s goal, and she did it with absolute, pure intentions.”

Christine reflected on seeing that Meri and Kody’s relationship fracture was beyond repair.

“They were struggling before that, for sure, but it was definitely the beginning of the real crumbling between the two of them,” she shared.

During Christine’s discussion with Meri — which marked their first one-on-one conversation since Christine left their plural family — she applauded Meri for being so accommodating with the legal divorce.

Meri Brown Texted Kody About Terminating Their Marriage — And Was Ghosted

“I thought it was for the good of the family,” Meri explained, which Christine totally understood.

However, Christine then confessed that she thought the divorce idea was “kind of planted,” pointing to “a couple of times” when Robyn and Kody both mentioned it in normal conversation.

“Kody’s dad legally divorced his mom to go look for another wife, legally married that wife. So Kody had seen that happen,” Christine told the cameras. “And then in Pinesdale, [Montana], is just another community of our previous church where Robyn mentioned that it happened.”

Where Does Sister Wives’ Kody Stands With Meri, Janelle, Christine and Robyn?

Christine, who shares six children with Kody, remembered sitting in Meri’s living room in Lehi, Utah, before the family moved and hearing Robyn say it was “beautiful” that this other plural wife “offered to get a legal divorce from her husband so he could legally marry another wife who was coming in with children.”

It was at that moment that Christine said she felt it was part of a bigger plan and that Robyn and Kody were trying to sway Meri into doing the same thing.

“I remember thinking, ‘What are you suggesting here? You think Meri should legally divorce Kody?’ I remember just thinking that and then it happened,” Christine recalled. “And then Meri’s like, ‘This is my idea. I’m coming up with this legal divorce.’ And I’m like, ‘No, you’re not.’”

Janelle agreed with Christine’s account of the situation, saying, “I think there was way more going on whether they were willing to admit it or not. I think so. I think they were wanting to go that direction.”

‘Sister Wives’ Recap: Janelle Says It’s ‘About Damn Time’ Meri Leaves Kody

Meri is still unsure as to how everything went down, telling the cameras, “I don’t know if they had a plan. If it wasn’t a plan, it worked very well in his favor.”

She confessed, “I definitely would have done things different when I divorced him had I been legally married to him. So I think he absolutely benefited. So he can call himself lucky.”

Meri remained in a spiritual union with Kody until January 2023. She was later granted a spiritual divorce from the Wyoming native.

Robyn, meanwhile, is still married to Kody but they are no longer a part of the show.

Phantom Blade Zero Crosses 4 Million Sales in 72 Hours

📅 Nov 02, 2026  |  Category: Science  |  Read Source →

After debuting with exceptionally strong concurrent player numbers on Steam and rave reviews, S-Game’s Phantom Blade Zero has sold over four million copies. Even better, it achieved this milestone within 72 hours.

The action RPG topped three million wishlists across PS5 and PC in September, and reportedly sold close to one million before launch (over that figure when accounting for other storefronts). Granted, it’s far below Game Science’s Black Myth: Wukong, which sold ten million copies in its first four days.

But it’s still an impressive milestone, especially compared to contemporaries like Control Resonant, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, and The Blood of Dawnwalker. On Steam, Phantom Blade Zero set a new peak concurrent player record yesterday at 833,921 and became the 14th highest-played title in the platform’s history. Not bad for what’s essentially a relatively unknown (if not new) IP.

For more details on Phantom Blade Zero, check out our review. We gave it a 9 for its combat, which starts a little slow but ramps up after the opening hours, whether it’s weapon selection or boss fights. The presentation is top-notch throughout, while the somewhat limited exploration (at least in the first two regions) expands significantly with interesting side quests and hidden bosses.
