Nintendo Reveals Surprise Pikmin Day Direct

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Nintendo has revealed a surprise Nintendo Direct for Pikmin Day because it has apparently decided that's a thing now.

Instead of spooky game announcements or horror movie memes, video game fans woke up to on Halloween 2026 was Mario creator Shigeru Miyamoto's face. That's because Nintendo has declared November 1 as Pikmin Day, and it's got a brand-new Direct to celebrate the occasion.

Yes, really. None of this is a joke. There's a little more than nine minutes of announcements to go over. Although there's no Pikmin 5 reveal or anything else too groundbreaking here, Miyamoto did take the time to share new information about Pikmin 4 and promised that, "going forward, I want people to know more about Pikmin."

Tomorrow is Pikmin Day!

Join in on the fun by playing Pikmin games on Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch. https://t.co/iYxZldya7p

— Nintendo of America (@NintendoAmerica) October 31, 2026

So, to start, why is the first of November Pikmin Day? Nintendo's miniaturized franchise launched in Japan in October 2001 before coming to North America in December, so there's no tie to a release date like you might have guessed. Instead, Miyamoto explained that November 1 is Pikmin Day because there were originally only three Pikmin, which, when standing next to each other, kinda looks like "11.1."

Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy

Ready to test your skills at Dandori Academy?

A free demo of #Pikmin4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy will be available on Nov 2. https://t.co/rNHNq35RMT

— Nintendo of America (@NintendoAmerica) October 31, 2026

You gotta give 'em credit for creativity, as Pikmin Day is probably one of the last things anyone expected to see today. Still, it served as a nice opportunity to offer an update on Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy ahead of its release November 12, 2026. Players will be able to use the new consoles built-in microphone to Oatchi, controlling them by using commands like 'rush' and 'go to work.'

The Dandori Academy mode, meanwhile, comes with new challenges, including exams that take place on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, that give players timed tasks and an opportunity to earn medals. A demo for the Switch 2 version of Pikimin 4 will be available starting November 2, with save data available to transfer to the full launch. It offers access to the Sun-Speckled Terrace opening area, as well as two Dandori Academy stages.

Pikmin Bloom

Secret Caves are coming this winter!

Get ready to explore Secret Caves, face off against familiar creatures, and uncover hidden treasures along the way in @PikminBloom on your smartphone. https://t.co/Nx6Lzba6v4

— Nintendo of America (@NintendoAmerica) October 31, 2026

Nintendo first brought its augmented-reality game, Pikmin Bloom, to smartphone devices in 2021, but five years on, and it's still got more to give. As shown in a teaser video featuring a group of the colorful plant-like creatures battling a Bulborb, an update scheduled to arrive this winter will add a new mechanic called Secret Caves. These battle-focused exploration events allow players to take their crew into caves to battle creatures and earn rewards.

Before a cave is explored, players must collect a cave map, which feature information about a cave and can be found on things like Postcards. Then, Pikmin are sent into the cave to search for treasures, with some of the items highlighted during the Direct including a rubber duck, a sword, a cookie, and a chef hat. Should a battle ensue, you'll command your army by sending them in to attack when the moment is right, or retreat if things get too hairy. Pikmin 4's Dandori Academy challenges also give players an opportunity to collect maps by taking exams.

Shigeru Miyamoto Really Likes Pikmin, But He's Not Alone

If you couldn't tell from the video games, shorts, and Easter Eggs Nintendo has highlighted through the franchise's 25-year history, it's got a soft spot for Pikmin. Miyamoto has spearheaded this charge, and because he wants people to know more about the company's adorable little mascots, he's enlisted some help.

Joining him during the Nintendo Direct broadcast was Japanese actress and crafty Pikmin fan Fuka Koshiba. Known for her work on TV projects like Siren and films like Lady Kaga, Miyamoto brought her in to help him "build more excitement around Pikmin going forward" after she shared hand-made Pikmin-themed hats on Instagram.

"Originally, I had heard on social media that you could get hats of the Red Pikmin, Blue Pikmin, and Yellow Pikmin from those claw machines," Koshiba said, holding one of her hats. "I'm not good at the claw machines, but I really wanted the hats, so I thought, 'Well, let's just knit them.'"

"I see," Miyamoto replied. "I'm also bad at the claw machines."

A New Hello! Pikmin Original Short Movie

That was a close one!

Watch Hello! Pikmin short movies and enjoy new Pikmin adventures. https://t.co/8OPB48hDZr

— Nintendo of America (@NintendoAmerica) October 31, 2026

The Pikmin Day Direct also featured a brand-new Pikmin Original Short Movie. Following last year's Close to You short, this video sees a Red Pikmin do its best to grab a baseball from a frog. It's as cute as you could ever expect.

As part of Nintendo's initiative to lean harder into the series, new shorts will be released monthly via the Nintendo Today! app, as well as other channels. However, there will also be a new episode released tomorrow, with another set to follow sometime in November. Miyamoto said he's collaborating with Nintendo Pictures staff to make the shorts.

That's all for the Pikmin Day Direct. While there was no hint at anything like Pikmin 5, planting November 1 as an official holiday is probably a sign that Nintendo has no intention of putting a cap on Pikmin content anytime soon. While we wait for any bigger updates on the franchise, you can check out IGN's 9/10 Pikmin 4 review.

La migliore pizzeria di Napoli è guidata dal Pizzaiolo dell’Anno

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Davide Ruotolo, il giovane pizzaiolo della pizzeria Palazzo Petrucci a piazza San Domenico, nel centro storico di Napoli, ha fatto l’en plein di vittorie e riconoscimenti in questo anno 2026.

A 30 anni appena compiuti ha una bacheca bella ricca. Uno sguardo al recente passato vede la proclamazione di Pizzaiolo Contemporaneo dell’Anno 2023 assegnato da Scatti di Gusto a Tuttopizza.

Ma è il 2026 che vede Ruotolo assoluto protagonista del mondo pizza e non solo napoletano.

In Campania ha vinto il nostro Campionato della Pizza 2026, dopo aver centrato il podio con il 2° posto nel 2025 e il 3° nel 2024. In pratica, in continua progressione, ha portato Palazzo Petrucci al titolo di migliore pizzeria di Napoli.

50 Top Pizza, in ambito nazionale, ha riconosciuto a Ruotolo lo stesso titolo. Podio con il 3° posto assoluto nella classifica Italia 2026 e migliore pizzeria di Napoli. Con il premio per la migliore scalata di posizioni: dal 22° al 3° posto e 19 gradini scalati.

Ancora, per Pizza Maxima 2026, Ruotolo sforna la migliore Pizza Creativa grazie alla sua Diavola che ha rivitalizzato una pizza tradizionale un po’ dimenticata.

Per Il Mattino Ruotolo è il migliore Pizzaiolo dell’Anno 2026

E ora, ultimo (almeno per il momento) riconoscimento con il premio speciale della Guida alle pizzerie della Campania de Il Mattino che gli assegna il titolo di Pizzaiolo dell’anno 2026.

Palazzo Petrucci Pizzeria ha 3 Galletti, il massimo punteggio, ma non c’è un voto di classifica (sono 24 le pizzerie di Napoli a fregiarsene). Quindi la combo 3 Galletti + premio Pizzaiolo dell’Anno diventa una carta pesante da giocare.

Il 2026 segna anche l’avvio di una nuova sfida per Ruotolo che ora si divide tra la pizzeria del centro storico di Napoli e la nuova apertura nel palazzo gastronomico sul mare di Posillipo. E, a giudicare dagli afflussi della pizzeria aperta al piano spiaggia di Palazzo Petrucci, i risultati sono ottimi. Almeno così dichiara il patron di Palazzo Petrucci, Edoardo Trotta, che ha ingaggiato un Ruotolo giovanissimo appena poco più che ventenne. E ha visto lungo.

I premi speciali della Guida alle pizzerie della Campania

La Guida de Il Mattino fotografa lo stato dell’arte della pizza in Campania con 350 indirizzi e una ristretta schiera di pizzerie che meritano i 3 Galletti. La selezione, operata da Luciano Pignataro, punta l’attenzione sul ritorno della pizza a ruota di carro. Un corso e ricorso della storia che suggerisce un trend a favore della pizza più tradizionale di Napoli. E che ora convive felicemente con le idee più contemporanee, quelle d’autore, la pizza al taglio e prossimamente la slice americana approdata a Milano.
